# LibreSky

<p align="center"><strong>The sky, in a browser tab.</strong></p>

LibreSky is a Bluesky client you open and stay in. Home is waiting. Discover is one click over. Search starts on what people are talking about right now. Chat, notifications, bookmarks, and your profile sit in the sidebar, and a voice or video call can leave straight from a conversation.

## Walk in

The first screen is sign-in. Make an account there if you need one. Bluesky’s own page takes the password. This browser keeps you signed in until you sign out.

You can browse before you sign in, or sign in with an app password. This browser holds up to eight accounts and switches between them.

**[Open LibreSky](https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/LibreSky/)**

## Everything in reach

| Place | What you find |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Following, Discover, and the lists and feeds you pin on that row |
| Search | Trending topics first, then people, posts, and feeds |
| Sidebar | Notifications, chat, bookmarks, your profile, calls, and AI Assist |
| More | Feeds, lists, starter packs, settings, About, Privacy, and Terms |
| Menu | Help and Config for the page you are on, plus downloads and support |

Posts come from your Bluesky account. The whole client is this site.

Help and Config follow the page. On Home they tune the feed. On a thread they set the reply order. On Search they set Latest or Top, people you follow, replies, languages, and sensitive posts.

## Write it

The composer takes images, a video, a link, a GIF, emoji, and a feed, list, or starter pack. Bold, italic, strikethrough, and monospace sit on the bar. The mic types what you say, in Chrome and Edge. Tab finishes a word, an `@` name, or a `#` tag.

A link that stands alone on the last line becomes the card. Edit posts the new version and removes the old one. Likes and replies stay on the original. An unsent draft follows the Bluesky account you are signed in to.

Who can reply is yours to set: everyone, people you follow, people you mention, or no one. Post languages ride on the post, up to three. Discover can stay inside the content languages you turn on. Thirty languages are listed in Settings.

## Say it out loud

Voice and Video sit on an open chat, on the Calls page, and on someone else’s profile. A call reaches another LibreSky window directly. Both people need the app open. The sound and the picture travel between the two browsers. Mute and the camera stay on your side. The time shows once the call connects. Hang up ends it.

An incoming call shows a banner. Accept or decline it there. Settings choose who can message you and who can call you: everyone, people you follow, or no one. The chat choice is your Bluesky setting. The call choice stays with LibreSky.

## Ask in the same breath

Type `@ai` or `@si`. Both tags ask the account you saved. Bluesky has no account behind either tag. In a chat, a message that contains the tag stays on your screen until you press Send. A tag in a post is only a highlight.

Settings can hold a Grok, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Together, Cerebras, Pollinations, or other account. Each row links to the site that issues a key. The key is saved on your Bluesky account, in the private preferences that follow this login. Anyone can read the public profile, so the key stays in those private preferences. The key is sent only to the provider you pick, and only when you ask, including a rewrite from the post box.

AI Assist also has its own page, an ask field on Home, and a check on profiles and posts. The spark on the post box rewrites the draft that is already there. Use this keeps the rewrite. Dismiss leaves your words. The [AI page](https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/LibreSky/#/ai) is the chat. Free, a remote AI, or Local is the button under the box. A key, an address, and a local model are set under Settings, on the AI tab. Without a key it uses a free public model so the page still answers.

## A desk you can tune

| Settings | What it keeps |
| --- | --- |
| You | Accounts, app passwords, who can message or call, and your profile |
| Follows | Follow people back while this tab is open, and clean up follows that look off |
| Safety | Muted words, muted accounts, and blocks |
| Display | Light, dark, and two high-contrast themes, larger post text, a screen-reader layout, and the keyboard |
| Feeds | Home, threads, and search |
| Languages | Content languages and the languages you post in |
| Sound | A volume for chat, notifications, and new posts, plus read-aloud |
| AI | Remote keys, a local model, and which one answers. The chat picks Free, a remote AI, or Local under the box |

Follow back can skip accounts that look like spam, and it pauses at a daily pace of 20, 40, or 100. Cleanup shows why an account looks off. Unfollow there holds the account, so follow back leaves it alone until you release it.

The speaker on a post reads the text and the image description. New posts on Home start quiet. The choice of theme, text size, and sound is saved on your account.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `n` | New post |
| `Shift` `Enter` | Send a post or a chat |
| `Tab` | Finish a word, `@` name, or `#` tag |
| `j` `k` | Next and previous post |
| `l` `r` `t` | Like, reply, repost |
| `b` `s` | Bookmark, share |
| `Enter` | Open the selected post |
| `/` | Search |
| `a` | Read the selected post aloud |
| `Shift` `↑` | Top of the page |

## A desk of your own

Linux, Windows, and Mac builds are on the [download page](https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/LibreSky/#/downloads). Unzip and run. Sign-in still uses your Bluesky account.

| System | File | First run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Linux | LibreSky-Linux.zip | Unzip the folder and run the LibreSky file |
| Windows | LibreSky-Windows.zip | Unzip the folder and run LibreSky.exe |
| Mac | LibreSky-Mac.zip | Unzip the folder, then right-click LibreSky.app and choose Open |

The Mac app is unsigned, so the first open is that right-click. Each button saves one zip. The page stitches the stored pieces into that file.

## Small enough to keep

The pages and scripts are about 1.5 MB. A service worker stores that shell, so the next open starts from this browser. A small Raspberry Pi can host them. The custom edition, with services you run yourself, is [LibreSky Corporate](CORPORATE.md). That one is coming soon.
