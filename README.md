# LibreSky

LibreSky is a Bluesky client. The app is this page:

https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/LibreSky/

Open that address. If you are not signed in, the page starts on a sign-in screen and then keeps that sign-in in this browser until you sign out. Create an account from that screen if you do not have one yet. Bluesky’s own page handles the password. Home, Discover, search, notifications, chat, bookmarks, and profiles stay in the sidebar. Starter packs are on Search. Feeds, lists, settings, About, Privacy, and Terms are in the More menu. Voice and Video sit on an open chat and on someone else’s profile. Posts come from your Bluesky account. A voice or video call reaches another LibreSky window directly. The AI item is not available yet.
