# LibreSky Corporate

![LibreSky Corporate, coming soon. Self-hosted services, small enough for a Pi.](assets/banner-corporate.png)

**Coming soon.**

LibreSky Corporate is the custom edition. You host it. The services run on hardware you keep, and the client is compact enough to live on a small Raspberry Pi.

## A sky on your own shelf

The public app is a Bluesky client you open in the browser: [LibreSky](https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/LibreSky/). Corporate is the build for a place that wants the services on a machine it owns. The client you would serve is about 1.6 MB of pages and scripts. A small Pi, on a desk or in a quiet corner of the network, can host it.

## What is on the way

- A custom LibreSky, shaped around services you host yourself
- The same compact client, small enough for a Raspberry Pi
- Your hardware, your services, your sky

A setup guide will land here when the edition is ready to run. The public client is open today, and the source is [on GitHub](https://github.com/TheWhiteWitchTM/LibreSky) under the MIT license.
